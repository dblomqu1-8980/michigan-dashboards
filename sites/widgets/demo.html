How it works

Your colours

We set the palette from your brand guide. Only the status colours stay fixed — they carry the forecast, not the styling.

One size that fits

A band in a wide column, a card in a narrow one. It reads the space it is given, so the same snippet works anywhere on the page.

Never a broken box

If a feed goes quiet it shows the last conditions it saw, with the time. If it cannot reach anything, it says so plainly and recovers on its own.

Nothing to maintain

Updates ship from our side. Your page keeps the one line you pasted, and no one on your team has to think about it again.
