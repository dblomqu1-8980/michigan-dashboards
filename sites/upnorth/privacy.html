Privacy Policy & Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: August 11, 2026 · Applies to upnorthdashboard.com ("Up North Dashboard")

The short version

This is a small, independent travel site covering Northern Lower Michigan. We don't sell your data, we don't require accounts, and we collect almost nothing. Some links on this site are affiliate links, which cost you nothing and help keep the dashboard running.

Affiliate disclosure

Up North Dashboard participates in affiliate advertising programs, including the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

We may also link to other affiliate partners, such as vacation rental platforms like VRBO. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase or booking, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. We only link to services we think are genuinely useful for a trip up north. Affiliate links carry rel="sponsored" in our code and are disclosed on the pages where they appear.

What we collect

  • Standard server logs. Like nearly every website, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and performance. We don't use this to identify individuals.
  • Local preferences. The dashboard stores a few display preferences — the manually edited "Today Up North" statuses and weekend event highlights — in your own browser's localStorage. That data never leaves your device and is never sent to us.

We do not require accounts, we do not collect payment information, and we do not sell, rent, or trade personal information with anyone.

Third-party data and services

Every live number on this dashboard comes from a public data source, fetched by your browser directly from that provider. Those providers may log the request under their own privacy policies:

  • National Weather Service / NOAA — observations, alerts, water levels, buoys
  • Open-Meteo — historical overnight lows, rainfall and snowfall
  • MDOT Mi Drive — road incidents, construction and traffic cameras (via our own Cloudflare Worker proxy)
  • Mackinac Bridge Authority — bridge restriction status and cameras
  • Michigan DNR — trail closure notices
  • U.S. Energy Information Administration — regional fuel prices
  • Unsplash — header photography
  • Amazon and VRBO — affiliate links

We encourage you to review those services' privacy policies if you have questions about their practices.

Accuracy and use of this site

Conditions change fast up north. Everything here is provided for planning purposes only and may be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. The fall-color percentages in particular are a model estimate, not a field survey. Always confirm road, ice, water, ferry and trail conditions with the responsible agency or operator before you travel, and never rely on this site for a safety decision.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience travel resource and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
