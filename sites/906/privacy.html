Privacy Policy & Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: July 10, 2026 · Applies to 906dashboard.com ("906 Travel Dashboard")

The short version

This is a small, independent travel site. We don't sell your data, we don't require accounts, and we collect almost nothing. If you join our email list, we use your address to send you aurora alerts and UP condition updates — that's it. Some links on this site are affiliate links, which cost you nothing and help keep the dashboard running.

Affiliate disclosure

906 Travel Dashboard is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

We may also link to other affiliate partners (such as vacation rental platforms like VRBO). If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase or booking, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. We only recommend products and services we believe are genuinely useful for UP travel. Affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored" in our code and disclosed on the pages where they appear.

What we collect

  • Email address (only if you sign up). Our signup forms are processed by FormSubmit.co, which forwards your submission to our email inbox. We use your address solely to send aurora alerts and UP condition updates. Unsubscribe anytime by replying to any email or emailing us directly, and we'll remove you promptly.
  • Standard server logs. Like nearly every website, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and performance. We don't use this to identify individuals.
  • Local preferences. Some dashboard features may store display preferences in your own browser (localStorage). This data never leaves your device.

We do not require accounts, we do not collect payment information, and we do not sell, rent, or trade personal information with anyone.

Third-party content and services

The dashboard displays live data and embedded content from third parties, which may set their own cookies or collect usage data under their own privacy policies:

  • NOAA / National Weather Service (weather, aurora, and water-level data)
  • MDOT and the Mackinac Bridge Authority (road conditions and cameras)
  • MarineTraffic (embedded live vessel map)
  • Amazon (affiliate links)
  • FormSubmit.co (email signup processing)
  • Unsplash (photography)
  • Google Tag Manager / Analytics (on some pages, to understand aggregate site usage)

We encourage you to review those services' privacy policies if you have questions about their practices.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience travel resource and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from emails at any time — just reply or email us.
  • Block cookies or clear localStorage in your browser without losing core site functionality.
  • Contact us to ask what information we hold about you or to request deletion.
